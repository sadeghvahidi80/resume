FAQ

Before you pay

How does payment work?

After choosing a service and filling the form, you are redirected to ZarinPal. On success you see a reference ID, and the order is stored against it.

How is the schedule agreed?

After payment I message you on the first working day to agree the start date and the first call. For project work, scope and timeline are finalized at that point.

What if my service is not listed?

Use the contact form. Larger or combined work is scoped first and then quoted.

Do you issue invoices?

For company projects, mention the invoice request in the contact form or the order note so it can be arranged.
