Story

The path, from content to systems

I'm Sadegh Vahidi — digital project manager and operational systems designer; founder of MATEN Technology Group.

I earned my BSc in Financial Management from the University of Tehran (2019–2022), and started my professional path in content production and market research — working with organizations and brands such as Ecell, Ronix, Poolaki, and Maktabkhooneh, focusing on content production and publishing workflows.

With every project, the question "how can this be made repeatable?" grew louder — and it took me from content to process design and systems building. Today at MATEN I manage research, knowledge management, automation, and digital development projects through a single point of contact: from problem framing and decision-making to trackable execution.
