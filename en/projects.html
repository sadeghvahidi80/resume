Projects & case studies

Tier 1

Six selected case studies

1 — Sabzevar home-appliance repair system (Karabi)
In plain terms
A clear path from request to delivery, so the customer always knows what stage the job is at.
Issue
Repair requests for home appliances were tracked without a central system; each stage was opaque to the customer.
Role
Requirements definition; development process management; work-queue structure.
Approach
A request-to-delivery path with stage-by-stage status and a dev work queue.
Tools
Requirements doc, process diagram, work queue.
Result
The system was designed and developed.
Demo
Open the demo (sample data)
2 — Multi-brand content management & production (Ronix, Poolaki, EscapeZoom, Maktabkhooneh)
In plain terms
A content factory: every article starts from a clear brief and ships at a consistent quality.
Issue
Specialized content for several brands across different fields — tools, accounting and finance, travel and entertainment, education — was produced without a unified process; quality was inconsistent and person-dependent.
Role
Designed the research-to-publish process; briefing; quality control; topic research and writing.
Approach
Standard brief → research → writing → quality control → publish, with process ownership; for blogs, research → writing → publishing with SEO structure.
Tools
Brief template, QC checklist, publishing calendar, keyword research, article templates.
Result
30+ tools articles (Ronix), 12+ accounting & finance articles (Poolaki), published EscapeZoom articles and Maktabkhooneh pieces — published on the brands’ own sites.
Documentation
Ronix: TIG vs MMA · Poolaki: best expense-tracking app · Poolaki: personal accounting with Excel · “Jenzadegi” article · “Tafrihat Shiraz” · Maktabkhooneh: SLN function in Excel
3 — Ensaf News: “The Problem of Agency: A Hazy Narrative of the Mongols”
In plain terms
A long-form story about why people feel powerless in history, grounded in historical examples.
Issue
The problem of social agency needed a transparent, evidenced media piece.
Role
Author of the feature; topic research and narrative structure.
Approach
An analysis of agency in Mongol-era narratives, with historical examples and a defined theoretical framework.
Tools
Topic research and narrative editing.
Result
Published feature on Ensaf News.
Documentation
ensafnews.com/647785 (Jun 2026)
4 — MATEN: architecture of a smart organization
In plain terms
Software assistants that take over repetitive work, with one human approval point before any sensitive action.
Issue
How can people and software assistants (agents) work together in a small company transparently, safely and repeatably?
Role
Founder — founding and operations management, alongside Mohammad Sangsfidi (Co-Founder & Chief Operating Partner).
Approach
An org chart and decision rules; a team of software assistants for repetitive work; and one human approval point before any sensitive decision.
Tools
Python, SQLite, n8n, and an assistant runtime (OpenCode) with 224 registered skills and a local router.
Result
6 internal processes launched (approvals, revenue, sales, automation, support, brain); 4 core modules active. This system has no public release.
Evidence
Sanitized console screenshot; the internal product name and customer identifiers are masked. View on Works
5 — Joint literary & cultural research (with Mohammad Sangsfidi)
In plain terms
Four analytical essays on Persian literature, each with a defined question and a theoretical frame.
Issue
A need for structured, framework-driven analytical research in Persian literature.
Role
Joint research and writing with Mohammad Sangsfidi; theoretical framing and text analysis.
Approach
Four completed studies: “Microfiction and satahat” (Winter 2022/23); “Evocative sounds in the story of Rostam and Esfandiyar,” Shahnameh (Winter 2022/23); “The exit of Tarabi through reader-response theory” (Spring 2023); “Mowlavi’s shift from narrator to preacher” (Jul 2023).
Tools
Theoretical text-analysis frameworks: Genette, reader-response theory, Grammont’s sound-texture theory.
Result
Four completed literary studies.
Evidence
The full document is available by email request; no public edition has been released.
6 — Ravan Yar platform
In plain terms
A bilingual website and Android app that connect people with therapists and financial supporters.
Issue
Access to psychotherapy for Persian speakers without full ability to pay, with no central system connecting seekers to therapists and sponsors.
Role
Design and development of the bilingual website, backend, server-side forms and admin panel; Android app development.
Approach
Farsi/English site on Node.js, with a core that does not depend on third-party commercial APIs, an admin panel and an Android app.
Tools
Node.js, responsive design, technical SEO, Jetpack Compose.
Link
The same independent nonprofit initiative listed in the resume.
Result
Version 2.0 is ready for release; the public build has not been published yet.
Evidence
The public build has not been released; demo with sample data.
