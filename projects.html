2 — Sabzevar repair-tracking system
Issue

Role
Requirements definition; development process management; work-queue structure.
Approach
A request-to-delivery path with stage-by-stage status and a dev work queue.

3 — Poolaki & Ronix content process
Issue
Specialized articles for tools and accounting were produced without a unified process; quality was inconsistent.
Role
Led the 3–4 person team; designed the research-to-publish process; briefing; quality control.
Approach
Standard brief → research → writing → quality control → publish, with process ownership.
Tools
Brief template, QC checklist, publishing calendar.
Result
30+ tools articles (Ronix) and 12+ accounting & finance articles (Poolaki) published.
Documentation
ronix.ir · poolaki.app

Role

Approach
Data collection → competitor analysis → a decision framework across 4 horizons.
Tools
Research report, competitor analysis, business model.
Result
Four-horizon market research report delivered.
